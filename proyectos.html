Eros: Immersive Music Experience

This music based experience was done in touch designer for an investigation project where we worked with the Colombian rapper "Neron Arkano" to protray his music on a inmersive experience projected onto a dome. The song worked as the reference and our mission was to use real time interactions from both the music and outside sources, how we could project the feeling and message of the song onto a dome.

On my case, i used the physical properties of sound, the live performance of Neron and values of variables controlled with a xbox controller to manipulate the animation, colors, cameras and post process effects to tell a story of love, desire, seduction and one-night stands.
